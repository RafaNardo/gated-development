# Decision Template

```md
# ADR-<number> — <Decision>

## Status
Proposed | Accepted | Superseded

## Context
What problem or constraint required a decision?

## Decision
What was chosen?

## Why
Why is this the preferred option now?

## Consequences
Positive:
- ...

Negative / trade-offs:
- ...

## Future trigger
What future event would justify revisiting this decision?
```

Record decisions that future agents need to understand. Avoid turning every implementation detail into an ADR.
